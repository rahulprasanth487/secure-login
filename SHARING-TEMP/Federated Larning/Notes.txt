PROBLEM STATEMENT:
Problem Statement – Secure AI in Banking
Banks are rapidly adopting LLM-powered assistants for customer service, fraud detection, and process automation.
However, these systems face critical challenges:

Prompt-based attacks (jailbreaks, phishing, adversarial queries) can bypass safeguards.

Model poisoning in federated training can inject bias or backdoors.

Inconsistent moderation across branches weakens fraud prevention.

Data privacy regulations prevent sharing raw customer data for model improvement.

Lack of verifiable audit trails makes regulatory compliance difficult.

Need: A privacy-preserving federated AI security framework that detects malicious prompts locally, leverages peer-reviewed classification, uses trust-weighted aggregation to resist attacks, and provides cryptographically verifiable audit logs.
